Our Story

Two homes,
one long morning.

More is one café with two front doors, the first in Marcellus, Michigan and the second in Kastav, Croatia. Each one brings the other a little of what it has been missing.

The USA

Marcellus,
Michigan

A small village in the southwest of the state, with lakes, orchards and farmland all around it.

Croatia

Kastav,
Croatia

An old hilltop town with stone streets and a view that runs all the way down to the Kvarner bay.

The Name

In English, more means more. In Croatian, more means the sea.

So in Michigan our name asks for another coffee, and in Kastav it points down the hill toward the water. We liked that one small word could say both, because that is exactly what we are trying to do.

From The Mediterranean To Michigan

What we bring to the USA

  1. 01

    Slowness

    The table is yours for as long as you like, and nobody brings the bill until you ask for it.

  2. 02

    Quality Of Food

    Good olive oil, ripe tomatoes, fresh bread and eggs, cooked simply so you can taste each of them.

  3. 03

    Atmosphere

    A terrace, a long table and an espresso that turns into two hours of talking.

  4. 04

    Nature

    Plants, open windows and fresh air, with as much of the meal as possible eaten outside.

  5. 05

    Marenda

    The late-morning meal of the Croatian coast, which is what brunch was called there long before brunch.

From Michigan To The Mediterranean

What we bring to Croatia

  1. 01

    Brunch Itself

    Most Croatian cafes serve only drinks, so all-day breakfast is already something new.

  2. 02

    American Plates

    Tall pancake stacks, bagels, eggs Benedict, cinnamon rolls.

  3. 03

    Michigan Flavours

    Blueberries, cherries, maple syrup, and apple cider with donuts in autumn.

  4. 04

    Coffee Habits

    Filter coffee with free refills, iced coffee, and cups to go.

  5. 05

    Seasonal Menus

    Pumpkin in autumn, a holiday menu in December.

Each café keeps a little of the other on the menu.
